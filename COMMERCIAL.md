# Commercial license

Castwire is dual-licensed:

| | AGPL-3.0 | Commercial license |
|---|---|---|
| Price | Free | Paid |
| Use in open-source apps (AGPL-3.0 or compatible) | Yes | Yes |
| Use in closed-source apps, SDKs or services | No: the AGPL requires your app's source to be published under the same license, including for apps offered over a network | Yes |
| Keep your changes to Castwire private | No | Yes |
| Priority answers and fixes | No | Yes |

## Who needs one

You need a commercial license if you ship Castwire, or anything built on it, in a product whose source
you don't publish under the AGPL-3.0. That includes:

- closed-source Android, desktop or TV apps;
- apps sold in stores where you don't publish the source;
- closed SDKs or libraries that include Castwire;
- server software using Castwire whose source you don't publish.

Personal projects, open-source apps under the AGPL-3.0 and evaluation (trying Castwire before deciding)
don't need one.

## How to get one

Open a [license request](https://github.com/biolend83/castwire/issues/new?labels=license&title=Commercial%20license%20request)
with your company name, the product, and the number of apps or developers. The details of the agreement
are worked out from there; nothing in the request is made public beyond its title.

The commercial license is a separate written agreement. This page describes it; it is not the agreement
itself.
